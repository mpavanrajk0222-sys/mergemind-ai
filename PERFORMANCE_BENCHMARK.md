# Performance Benchmark

This summary is transcribed from the checked-in [benchmark report](reports/PERFORMANCE_BENCHMARK.md) and its machine-readable [JSON results](reports/performance_benchmark.json), generated 2026-11-14. The measurement did not use the project database. Only one of four full pipeline attempts completed; all 100K attempts are incomplete and must not be presented as successful processing results.

## Method

- Synthetic customer/product inputs generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` with seed 42, at 10,000 and 100,000 rows per entity.
- Customer and product runs measured separately. Database, report, and log artifacts used OS temporary directories.
- Process-tree RSS was sampled by `psutil` every 100 ms. The harness stopped an attempt when sampled RSS crossed 1,250 MiB or after 180 seconds.
- Completed timing data comes from pipeline reports; partial stages come from structured logs. Wall time includes CLI/harness overhead and is distinct from pipeline execution duration.
- Environment: Windows 10 build 26200, Python 3.10.11, 4 logical CPUs, Intel64 Family 6 Model 142 Stepping 12. Physical RAM was not recorded.

## Completed Customer 10K Details

- Pipeline duration: 28.281335 seconds; throughput: 353.59 ingested input rows/second.
- Peak sampled RSS: 445,808,640 bytes (425.16 MiB).
- Probable matching: 195,808 evaluated candidates out of 49,000,050 theoretical accepted-record pairs; 99.60% blocking reduction; about 46,849 candidates/second.
- SQLite persistence stages totaled 21.674694 seconds.
- Duplicate-decision persistence alone: 16.598707 seconds for 195,907 persisted decision rows.
- Master/lineage persistence: 4.365766 seconds.
- Correctness checks: SQLite `integrity_check` returned `ok`; foreign-key check had zero violations; report/database counts reconciled; all 9,900 accepted source IDs appeared exactly once among 9,701 masters; 100 rejected rows remained source rows and quarantine entries; master IDs were unique.

## Partial Product 10K Details

Probable matching took 50.743295 seconds and evaluated 1,240,398 candidates (97.47% reduction): 917 strong, 28,555 review-required, and 1,210,926 no-match. The run generated/persisted 29,303 review items and 100 quarantine entries. It crossed the guard at 75.591 seconds while `persist_duplicate_decisions` was active, with 1,240,581 decisions submitted to that stage. It did not complete and has no end-to-end reconciliation claim. The JSON benchmark records 14.131260 seconds for completed SQLite persistence stages before interruption, not the total persistence time.

## 100K Resource-Limit Results

Both 100K runs ingested 100,000 rows (99,000 accepted, 1,000 rejected), normalized them, and completed exact detection. Customer found 999 exact groups; product found 922. Both stopped as probable matching began, before candidate totals returned and before final result persistence.

## Bottlenecks and Limitations

1. Candidate-pair materialization/scoring is the primary scaling bottleneck. Product 10K produced more than 1.24 million blocked candidates and reached approximately 1.25 GiB RSS; both 100K attempts crossed the guard as probable matching began.
2. Duplicate-decision persistence is a secondary cost. Customer 10K spent 16.599 seconds writing 195,907 decisions; all SQLite write stages used 21.675 seconds.
3. Review volume can also be significant: product 10K wrote 29,303 review rows in 11.474 seconds.
4. RSS is sampled and may miss short peaks or overshoot the guard between samples.
5. The benchmark uses a fixed synthetic distribution with injected duplicate and invalid scenarios. It does not estimate real-world prevalence or matching accuracy.
6. No matching or persistence optimization was applied from this benchmark. A streaming candidate design would need separate correctness/accuracy-equivalence evaluation.

The benchmark report records its own contemporaneous test run as **215 passed, 1 warning**. That is a historical benchmark snapshot, not a count of the current test suite.
